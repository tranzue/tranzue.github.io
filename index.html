SondeHub

A radiosonde_auto_rx receiver running on a Raspberry Pi, decoding the twice-daily weather-balloon launches out of NWS Pittsburgh and feeding them to SondeHub as KD3CLW. The map below is my node — whatever it's hearing right now.

Receiving NWS Pittsburgh sondes · full tracker at sondehub.org

shipped · iOS & watchOS

HamBands

An iPhone and Apple Watch app that reads NOAA solar data and tells you which HF bands are actually open before you key up. Trend charts, a greyline view, watch complications, and home-screen widgets. Built with the POTA crowd in mind.

App Store source
building · WSPR

Pico balloon

A Traquito Jetpack build — a solar-powered WSPR beacon light enough to float a tiny station around the planet and report its own position home. On the bench for now; not yet aloft.

Wardriving

The radio doesn't stop at HF when I'm on the road. I like to wardrive, logging the Wi-Fi around me as I go and adding it to a shared map. My sightings count toward WDGWars, a community wardriving game and leaderboard. Here's where my tally stands.
