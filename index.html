SondeHub receiver

Live feed

A radiosonde_auto_rx receiver on a Raspberry Pi, decoding the twice daily weather balloon launches out of NWS Pittsburgh and feeding them to SondeHub as KD3CLW. The map is whatever my node is hearing right now.
