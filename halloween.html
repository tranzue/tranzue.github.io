Talking points

  1. Reese's is still king, but losing its grip

    Top item both years, yet the only big name that went down while the total went up. Nearly 1 in 5 pieces in 2025; about 1 in 8 this year.

  2. The Hershey's Bar was the breakout star

    From 11 to 31, nearly triple, jumping from mid-pack to #2. Somebody on the route bought a whole case.

  3. The Whoppers house

    Whoppers went from 1 to 12, a 12x increase. Almost certainly one generous neighbor rather than a trend.

  4. The haul got more even

    The top three made up 37.5% of everything in 2025, but only 31% in 2026. More variety, less dominance by the usual favorites.

  5. Salty snacks nearly doubled

    Goldfish, Cheeseballs, Pretzels, Doritos and Chex Mix went from 17 pieces to 31, led by Cheeseballs (3 → 12) and Doritos (1 → 6).

  6. Mars had a rough year on chocolate

    Snickers, M&Ms and Twix all dropped. Mars' fruity side went the other way: Starburst 2 → 9 and Skittles 1 → 5.

  7. Hershey's grip tightened

    Everything Hershey makes went from about 39% of the haul to 43%. Fitting for a Pennsylvania trick-or-treat route.

  8. Fruit Snacks crashed

    From 14 to 6, the biggest drop of anything. Lollipops slipped too, from 20 to 15.

  9. The neighborhood got fancier and healthier

    Ghirardelli showed up for the first time, applesauce pouches tripled, and Rice Krispy Treats more than tripled (6 → 19).

  10. Who disappeared

    Sour Gummy Worms, Cheeto Puffs and Hard Fruity Candy dropped off, and Nerds Gummy Clusters went from 4 to zero. York Mints and Chex Mix were new arrivals. Rolo, Pretzels and Almond Joy came in at exactly the same count both years.
